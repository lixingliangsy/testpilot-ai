# TestPilot — Use cases

| Segment | Pain | How TestPilot helps |
|---|---|---|
| Operations & compliance leads | Need a repeatable eval report without hiring a specialist | TestPilot turns raw input into a reviewed, exportable result in minutes |
| Small teams & agencies | Lack bandwidth to do eval report manually for every client | Self-serve: paste input, get a structured deliverable and share it |
| Founders & indie builders | Want a defensible, repeatable eval report step in their product | TestPilot is the drop-in layer they can white-label or embed |
