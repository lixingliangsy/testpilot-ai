# TestPilot — FAQ

**Q: What is TestPilot?**
A: Paste a prompt and expected behavior; get an eval checklist and version regression guard. For dev and QA teams shipping LLM-powered product features.

**Q: Do I need an API key?**
A: No. The platform key runs the engine; Enterprise offers BYOK.

**Q: How is my data used?**
A: Only to produce your eval report. We do not train public models on it without consent.

**Q: Can I export the result?**
A: Yes — JSON download and clipboard on every plan.

**Q: Is there a free tier?**
A: Yes — a daily trial run, watermarked export.
